# Aerion DAW v0.5.3 Alpha (in progress)

Three more Milestone 6 items (a spectrum analyser and phase correlation meter, default MIDI mappings, and a decision on CLAP plugins) and fixes found while testing v0.5.2.

## New

### Meters: spectrum and phase correlation
**View › Loudness Meter** is now **View › Meters**, with two more readings of what you hear:

- **Phase correlation** shows whether your mix holds up in mono: +1 is mono, around 0 is wide stereo, and below 0 (shown in red) means parts of the mix cancel on a mono speaker.
- **Spectrum** shows the level of each frequency from 20 Hz to 20 kHz, so too much low end or a harsh peak stands out.
- The window can now be resized.

### Default MIDI mappings
Set up your controller once. Map the controls in a project, then click **Save as Default** in **Audio › MIDI Mappings…**. New projects start with your master mappings, and each new track gets the mappings of the track in its position. Defaults cover master and track faders and pan; plugin mappings stay in their project. **Clear Defaults** removes them.

### CLAP plugins: not yet
We looked at hosting CLAP plugins. The libraries Aerion is built on (JUCE and Tracktion Engine) can't host CLAP yet, so Aerion stays with VST3 (and Audio Units on macOS) for now. Most CLAP plugins also come as VST3.

## Fixed

- **The Inspector updates when you drop a plugin on the master.** Dragging a plugin from the Browser onto the master bus (or any track) added it, but the Inspector only showed it after you clicked into the Inspector. Adding or removing a plugin now refreshes the Inspector, the Mixer and the Timeline straight away.
- **No more blue frame after clicking BYP.** Clicking a button in the Inspector, such as BYP on a plugin, drew a blue frame around the Inspector's fader. It was the keyboard focus frame: the click put keyboard focus on the fader. A click now leaves focus on the Inspector itself; the frame appears only when you move to the fader with the keyboard.
- **The Export Mixdown window no longer freezes when you change the tail length.** Dragging the tail slider started a new preview render for every step and waited, with the window locked, for the previous one to stop; with heavy plugins on the master this could hang Aerion ("Not Responding"). The preview now renders once when you let go of the slider, and a preview being replaced stops in the background.
- **No "Rendered" message for the preview.** Each finished preview in the Export Mixdown window was treated like a finished export and showed a "Rendered:" message pointing at a temporary file. The preview now just shows its waveform.
