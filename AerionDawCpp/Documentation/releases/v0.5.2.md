# Aerion DAW v0.5.2 Alpha

Three new tools from Milestone 6 (track templates, MIDI learn and a loudness meter) and two fixes. This release also replaces v0.5.1.1, which the in-app updater could not see.

## New

### Track templates
Save any track as a template, with its plugins, their settings and its sends, and add it to any project in one step.

- **Save:** right-click a track header (or a Mixer strip) and choose **Save Track as Template…**. Clips are left out unless you tick *Include clips*. For a folder, the template includes all the tracks inside it.
- **Insert:** **Track › Insert from Template** adds it after the selected track. You can insert the same template as often as you like.
- **Show Track Templates Folder** in the same menu opens the folder, so you can copy templates between computers.

### MIDI learn
Control a fader or a plugin knob from your MIDI controller.

- **Fader or pan:** right-click the fader in the Mixer or Inspector, then choose **MIDI Learn › Learn Volume** (or **Learn Pan**). Then move a knob or fader on your controller.
- **Any plugin control:** choose **Audio › MIDI Learn**, move the control in the plugin's window, then move the hardware control.
- While Aerion waits for a controller, a **LEARN** pill shows in the toolbar; click it to cancel. Learning stops by itself once a control is mapped.
- **Audio › MIDI Mappings…** lists every mapping in the project and lets you remove them. A mapped fader shows a small **M**.
- Mappings are saved with the project.

### Loudness meter
**View › Loudness Meter** measures what you hear, after the master plugins and the master fader:

- **Integrated loudness** for the whole song, plus **short-term** (3 s) and **momentary** (400 ms) loudness, in LUFS.
- **True-peak** maximum in dBTP.
- **Targets** for Spotify and YouTube (−14 LUFS), Apple Music (−16), EBU R128 broadcast (−23) and ATSC A/85 (−24). The integrated value turns blue on target, red when too loud and yellow when too quiet. The true peak turns red above the target's ceiling.
- Integrated loudness and the true peak only count while the song plays. **Reset** starts a new measurement.
- The master strip in the Mixer shows the short-term loudness at all times.

It follows the ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128 method. It measures only the first two output channels, and it includes the metronome when the metronome is on.

## Fixed

- **Dragging from the Browser is tidier.** Dragging a plugin or an audio file used to drag a picture of the whole Browser panel. It now drags a small chip with the name.
- **The master stays selected in the Inspector.** Clicking BYP on a master insert, or adding or removing one, made the Inspector fall back to "(no selection)". It now keeps showing the master.

## Updating

Aerion offers this update itself (**Help › Check for Updates**, or shortly after it starts). If you installed v0.5.1.1, you will be offered v0.5.2 too.

Projects from v0.5.1 open unchanged.
