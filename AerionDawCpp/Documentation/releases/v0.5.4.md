# Aerion DAW v0.5.4 Alpha

Full automation: any setting of a track or plugin, in as many lanes as you like, drawn or recorded while the song plays. This completes another Milestone 6 item. Also: a lighter playhead in the piano roll.

## New

### Automate any setting
Until now automation covered a track's volume and pan. Now a lane can show any setting on the track: volume, pan, a send level, or any parameter of a plugin. Click a lane's name to choose; the menu lists the plugins and sends, and marks settings that already have automation with a dot. Plugin settings show their own values and units, and on/off settings snap to their steps.

### Several lanes per track
Each lane has **+** to add a lane below it and **×** to remove it, and you can drag a lane's bottom edge to make it taller or shorter. **Show Automated Parameters** (in a lane's menu, or under **Automation** in the track's right-click menu) opens a lane for everything on the track that already has automation. The lanes you open, their order and their heights are saved with the project, and they come along when you insert a track template.

### Record automation: Read, Touch, Latch and Write
Each track has an automation mode, on the new button after **A** in its header:

- **Read** (the default) plays automation back and records nothing.
- **Touch** records a control while you hold it, and lets go when you do.
- **Latch** records from your first move until playback stops.
- **Write** records every setting with a lane on the track from the start of playback until it stops, replacing what was there.

Anything that moves a setting records: the faders and pan knobs in the Mixer and Inspector, a MIDI controller mapped with MIDI learn, and the controls in a plugin's own window. You see the curve appear in the lane as you play. A plugin window or a controller can't tell Aerion when you let go, so in Touch mode their recording stops two seconds after the last move.

### Thin and clear automation
**Thin Automation** in a lane's menu removes points the curve doesn't need, so a recorded pass is easy to edit; the shape stays within 1 % of the lane's height. **Clear Automation** removes all of a lane's points. Both can be undone.

### Automation follows clips
Moving a clip on the Timeline now takes the automation under it along, later or earlier in the song and onto another track. Turn this off with **Audio › Automation Follows Clips**.

## Improved

- **Piano roll playback is lighter.** The playhead is now drawn over a saved image of the notes, so each step of playback copies two thin strips instead of redrawing every note under the line: about 18 times less work with the software renderer in our measurements.
- **Screen readers reach the new controls:** the automation mode button and each lane's parameter, + and × buttons.

## Fixed

- **Dragging a point in an automation lane** no longer counts as moving the setting itself, so editing a curve during playback can't start a recording.

## Good to know

- Switching a track to **Read** while the song plays stops all recording in progress, on every track; the others start again with their next move.
- There's no copy and paste of clips yet, so automation follows clips only when they are moved.
